Template Demo

This is a full-page template. You can mix IWML and HTML however you like—go wild, Frankenstein your layouts together. This one is simple on purpose, but you can plug in the most overengineered Bootstrap monsters too (Don't trust us, check out the AdminLTE demo for proof).

In addition to the Template.iwml and Template.ts/js, you will also want to look at Template_.html which contains the HTML that gets blended in.
